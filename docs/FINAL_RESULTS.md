## 1. Primary and secondary claims

The study question (plan 0.1) is about ER first: the plan named **ER 1a and 1b as primary** on 13 Oct 2026, before the
frozen-slide data existed, and 1c applies the same ER question to the frozen slide. Everything else is secondary.

**Primary model.** UNI v1 (the plan's main encoder), mean-pooled patch features + logistic regression (C = 0.01,
balanced), fixed site-grouped splits, 5 seeds. It is the only head that exists for every experiment (1a-1e) on the fixed
splits. TITAN, CONCH and the ABMIL heads are **replications**: a claim needs the same direction in at least two
encoders (decision rule 0.5).

| P1 | 1a: the RNA-defined ER label is more predictable from H&E than the IHC label | ΔAUC +0.036 (DeLong 95% CI +0.007, +0.065), p = 0.014, n = 801 | Same direction in all 5 variants; CI excludes 0 in all 5 | **Supported** |

## 2. Multiple-testing note

Across 1a-1e the project computes roughly **80 p-values or CIs**: 28 paired ΔAUC comparisons (5 label pairs × 5 model
variants + 3 prevalence-matched PR), ~23 per-variant 1b binomial tests plus the combined-evidence rows, 10 HER2-low
AUCs, 4 1c interactions and 6 1b tests on frozen vs FFPE slides, and 2 1e correlations. At α = 0.05, about **4 false
positives are expected by chance** if no effect existed anywhere.

How this is handled:
1. Only P1-P3 are confirmatory, with Holm correction (Section 1).
2. Secondary results are reported with **all** variants and never selected by significance; a single-variant or
   single-test result with p between 0.01 and 0.05 (for example TITAN's prevalence-matched PR ΔAUC, p = 0.032, or the
   ER-RPPA 1c interaction, p = 0.045) is labelled **not robust**.
3. Subgroups with n < 20 (ER-low, HER2-enriched exceptions) are leads, never conclusions.
4. All splits, labels and cut-offs were fixed before the frozen-slide results (plan 0.3); the cut-off robustness and
   negative controls (shuffled labels, site-only models) bound how much of any effect could be leakage.
